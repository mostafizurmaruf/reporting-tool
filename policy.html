VERSATILO GROUP

PRIVACY POLICY

How Versatilo Tool collects, uses, stores and protects your information.

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. Who we are

Versatilo Tool (“the Tool”, “we”, “us”) is a browser-based workspace used by Versatilo Group staff to prepare daily task reports, edit report documents and generate app icons. The Tool is a static website: it has no server and no database of its own. Everything it does runs inside your browser.

2. Information the Tool handles

  • Report content you type or import – employee name, ID, designation, department, manager, dates, office times, tasks and remarks. This is saved only in your browser’s local storage so your draft survives a page reload.
  • Settings – your theme choice, GitHub username / token, Gemini API key and Google Docs / Sheets links. These stay in your browser’s local storage and are never sent to us.
  • Google access token – when you sign in with Google, a short-lived access token is kept in local storage until it expires (about one hour) or you sign out.
  • Files you open in Edit Document or Icon Generator – processed in memory in your browser. They are never uploaded anywhere by the Tool.

We do not use cookies for tracking, analytics or advertising, and we do not collect any information on a server.

3. Google user data

If you choose “Sign in with Google”, the Tool asks for these permissions:

  • documents.readonly – to read the Google Docs you link in Settings, so their tasks can be turned into report rows.
  • spreadsheets.readonly – to read the Google Sheets you link in Settings, for the same purpose.
  • drive.file – to save the PDF / DOCX reports you download into a folder named “Versatilo Daily Reports” in your own Google Drive, and to list and download those same files on the History page. This permission only covers files the Tool itself created; it cannot see the rest of your Drive.

Google data is requested directly from your browser to Google’s APIs. It is shown to you, used to fill your report, and is not sent to our servers (we have none), sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

Versatilo Tool’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Third-party services you can turn on

Some features send data from your browser straight to another provider, only when you use them:

  • Google Docs, Sheets & Drive – as described above.
  • Google Gemini – if you add your own Gemini API key and use an AI action, the related report text is sent to Google’s Gemini API with your key. On the Image to Text page, choosing the Gemini AI engine sends the images you pick to Gemini the same way.
  • GitHub – if you add a GitHub username / token, the Tool reads your commit history from GitHub’s API to suggest tasks.
  • Content delivery networks – the Edit Document and PDF Converter pages load open-source libraries (pdf.js, pdf-lib) from cdnjs.cloudflare.com, the Video Compressor page loads Mediabunny and the Image to Text and PDF Converter pages load Tesseract.js and its language data from cdn.jsdelivr.net; these providers can see your IP address when serving them. Your videos, images and documents are processed in your browser and are never sent to them.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy.

5. Where your data is stored

On your device (browser local storage) and, if you enable Drive saving, in your own Google Drive. Nothing is stored by Versatilo Group outside of those places.

6. Your choices and deleting data

  • Clear your browser’s site data for this Tool to remove every saved draft, setting and token.
  • Turn off Drive saving in Settings at any time.
  • Delete saved reports from the “Versatilo Daily Reports” folder in Google Drive.
  • Revoke the Tool’s Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Revoke a GitHub token at github.com/settings/tokens.

7. Security

All connections to Google, GitHub and the CDN use HTTPS. Because tokens and keys are kept in your browser, keep your device and browser account secure and do not use the Tool on shared computers without clearing site data afterwards.

8. Children

The Tool is a workplace tool and is not intended for anyone under 16.
